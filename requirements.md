# SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS!
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

### Digital face: 
 A1: The robot will have animated eyes and face experessions.
 A2: The face experssion should support: idle, thinking, listening, angry, happy, sad, distrubed, curious, active, noraml, and disconnected.
 A3: Display module will be replaceable. (If something wrong happens with it).

 ### Conversations
 A4: The Robot will get the input from a mic, send the voice input to cloud and cloud will send the appropriate response.
 A5: The system will provide customizeable personality directives.
 A6: The system will make sure it is not saying something inappropriate, vulgur or something like tht.

 ### Visual Awereness: 
 A7: The robot will have camara (faced to the front)
 A8: The robot will send answers according to what it sees.
 A9: There will be a option or settings so the user can understand the camara is active
 A10: Images will be captured and transmitted in accordance with documented privacy controls.

 ### MOVEMENT (Importnat)
 A11: The Head will move in Horizantal direction and it should be able to tilt.
 A12: We'll add extra limb movement after the first head mechanism test.
 A13: The microcontroller or the Embedded system must be able to do its own movement even if offline and not relyingh on Cloud or AI entirely for it.

 ### Wearing of it
 A14: The robot will get attched to the shoulder through mount or something liek that.
 A15: The mount will have non-slipable material.
 A16: The mount shall be able to support the robot with no actuators.
 A17: The robot will be easily moveable from the shoulder to somewhere else easily without too much working hard. Like attaching it and removing it from your shoulder or from somewhere in 10-15secs easily.
