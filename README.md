# Introduction

LunaRobo, it is a little humaniod robot which is made to sit in your shoulder like a parrot. Express Expresions through OLED display, and communicate with its surroundings.
- - - - - - - - - - - - - 
# What is it?
It is a wearable robotic device or Companion that can talk, see, Communicate, and express its expersion in display.

# Why it is different from other AI tools?
Unlike other AI tools like Chatgpt, Gemini etc, it can securely sit in your shoulder, view the surrondings with a camara, it can interect with Visual experessions and Voice.

# Features
(i) Face: It will be able to express its expressions in a screen and will have animated face, eye blinking and some more expression types.
(ii) Conversion: It will be connected to AI for proper communication and interaction.
(iii) Visual Awereness: It will be able to see its surrondings with the help of its camara and understand.
(iv) Personality Mecanism: Reactions, Humor, etc.
(v) Movement: It will be able to move itself, some of its body part, like tilting in a certain angle, Moving its Head or Small limbs.
(vi) Wearable: We will be able to wear it unlike other robots like Emo and others.
(vii) Helping: It will be able to answer your questions, help you with your homework, find materials etc.

# How it will be:
First of all Cloud AI will be there for conversions, image recognition, etc. 
Then with the help of the Custom PCB, it will be able to manage AI requests, get audio processing, send signlas to bodyparts and act like a brain. Handling of servo movements will be also handled by the microcontroller.

# Project goal (Why I am making it)
The main goal of the project is to build a working, wearable AI type companion that can talk, interect, react, help, hold conversions, and see the world independently in a physical form.
